What is Mankind Minds?

A place for the people behind the work.

Mankind Minds is a space for creators to show what they make and for everyone else to discover the people behind it. Tattoo artists, visual artists, musicians, writers and video creators can build a profile and share their work.

As generated content becomes harder to spot, we want to help creators be open about how their work is made. Our AI-free verification process is one way to build that confidence, without pretending that any single test tells the whole story.

What do we believe in?

It's not humans versus technology.

We're not against every use of artificial intelligence. It can do extraordinary things. Take AlphaFold, which helps researchers predict the shapes of proteins and has opened up new possibilities for understanding disease and developing treatments.

What worries us is generative AI replacing the human creativity, practice and imagination that make art meaningful. We believe people deserve to know whether what they're looking at was made by a person or generated by a machine.

There's an environmental cost to consider too. The data centres behind AI need electricity and resources to operate. As AI grows, so does the importance of understanding its impact. The International Energy Agency's research is a useful place to learn more.

For us, it's about making room for human ideas, being honest about how work is made, and celebrating the people who put themselves into it.

For every category

Then, there's the human part.

Across all five categories, uploaded images are checked by the same self-hosted AI detector for signs of AI generation. If the result is uncertain or the check can't be completed, the image is held for our team to review. That's why some profiles or new portfolio items can take a little longer to appear.

Videos and text aren't put through the image detector; they go to our team for review. We also review creator applications. No detector or review can prove with absolute certainty that work is AI-free, so we consider the information available rather than promising a perfect result.

Our detector runs on our own hosted service, not on OpenAI or Google's image-generation services. It processes images for screening rather than keeping them as portfolio records. The site separately stores portfolio uploads and screening results to run profiles and reviews. Read our privacy policy for more about how we handle data.

About the creators

Hi, we're Dani and Thomas.

We're university students, and we've both always loved being creative. Mankind Minds started with a moment that stuck with us: a friend got a tattoo based on an AI-generated image, and it made us stop and think about how easily the person behind a piece of work can disappear from view.

We realised we wanted to do something about it. So we're building a place where creators can share what they make, be open about their process, and find people who care about the human side of creativity as much as we do.

Frequently asked questions

Questions about AI-free verification

What is Mankind Minds?

Mankind Minds is a creator platform with portfolio profiles and an AI-free verification process. It helps people discover creators and learn more about the human work behind their portfolios.

How can I verify my art as AI-free?

Create an account, complete your profile and submit an application with your work and details about your practice. Our team reviews the submission; if it is approved, you can share your verified profile.

How can I show that I didn't use AI?

Context such as sketches, drafts, work-in-progress material, project files and an explanation of your process can help demonstrate how you made a work. Keep these records where possible. No single form of evidence or automated detector is a perfect proof.

Does Mankind Minds use an AI detector?

Images are screened using our self-hosted detector for indicators of AI-generated content. Uncertain or unavailable results are held for our team to review. Videos and text are not run through the image checker; they are held for staff review.

Can you guarantee that a work is AI-free?

No. Automated checks and human review have limitations. Verification is based on the information available to us and should not be treated as absolute proof.

Can I update my portfolio after approval?

Yes. You can update your profile and add new work. New items may be held for review before they appear publicly; already approved items can remain visible.

Who can apply?

We welcome tattoo artists, visual artists, musicians, writers and video creators who want to share their human-made work. Create an account and choose the category that best describes your practice.
