What is Mankind Minds?

A place for the people behind the work.

Mankind Minds is a platform for creators who want to showcase human-made work and connect with people who value it. We bring together tattoo artists, visual artists, musicians, writers and video creators through creator profiles and an AI-free verification process.

As generated content becomes more common, it can be harder for audiences to understand how something was made. Our goal is to make the creator and their craft easier to recognise, without pretending that a single test can tell the whole story.

Our review

How to verify your art as AI-free

We combine the context you provide with review tools and decisions by our team. Our process continues to develop as the technology and creative landscape change.

01

Create your profile

Create an account, verify your email and add your bio, profile picture, links and portfolio. Complete the application so our team can learn about you and your work.

02

Share your work

Submit your portfolio and tell us about your practice. Images are screened for possible AI-generated content; video and text are reviewed by staff rather than through the image checker.

03

Review and decision

Our team reviews submissions and follows up with a decision. Approved creators can share their profile and QR code. New portfolio items may be held for review before becoming visible.

No detector or review can prove with absolute certainty that a work is AI-free. Our decisions reflect the information available at the time, not an infallible guarantee.

The people and the purpose

Made for creators, and the people who find them.

Behind every portfolio is a person: an artist refining a drawing, a musician building a sound, a writer finding the right words, or a filmmaker shaping a story. Mankind Minds exists to make space for the skill, experience and individual choices that go into that work.

Our team is building a community where creators can present their practice and audiences can discover work made by people. We want our standards and tools to improve with the community, while keeping human judgement and transparency at the heart of the process.

Frequently asked questions

Questions about AI-free verification

What is Mankind Minds?

Mankind Minds is a creator platform with portfolio profiles and an AI-free verification process. It helps people discover creators and learn more about the human work behind their portfolios.

How can I verify my art as AI-free?

Create an account, complete your profile and submit an application with your work and details about your practice. Our team reviews the submission; if it is approved, you can share your verified profile.

How can I show that I didn't use AI?

Context such as sketches, drafts, work-in-progress material, project files and an explanation of your process can help demonstrate how you made a work. Keep these records where possible. No single form of evidence or automated detector is a perfect proof.

Does Mankind Minds use an AI detector?

Images are screened using automated checks for indicators of AI-generated content, alongside review by our team where needed. Videos and text are not run through the image checker; they are held for staff review.

Can you guarantee that a work is AI-free?

No. Automated checks and human review have limitations. Verification is based on the information available to us and should not be treated as absolute proof.

Can I update my portfolio after approval?

Yes. You can update your profile and add new work. New items may be held for review before they appear publicly; already approved items can remain visible.

Who can apply?

We welcome tattoo artists, visual artists, musicians, writers and video creators who want to share their human-made work. Create an account and choose the category that best describes your practice.
